## 🧬 About Me

I'm a Bioinformatician and Data Scientist working on pangenomics, structural variants, and GWAS in livestock genomes. My work connects animal breeding to computational biology to address questions such as: which genomic variants explain adaptation in tropical cattle?

**What I do**

- Build multi-assembly pangenome graphs from ONT and PacBio HiFi long reads
- Call and genotype structural variants at population scale (short + long reads)
- Run GWAS and prioritize candidate loci for complex and functional traits
- Develop reproducible pipelines for HPC clusters (Snakemake, SLURM)
- Translate genomic results into decisions for breeding programs and non-technical stakeholders

**Academic background**

- 🎓 **Postdoctoral researcher and leader of the COWADAPT Project**, FMVZ/USP (2023–2026)
- 🎓 **PhD in Genetics and Animal Breeding**, FCAV/UNESP (2023)
- 🎓 **MSc in Zootechnics (Genetics & Animal Breeding)**, UESB (2019)
- 🎓 **MBA in Agribusiness**, ESALQ/USP (2015)
- 🎓 **DVM in Veterinary Medicine**, PUC Minas (2012)

**I am interested** in computational biology, genomics, and precision livestock farming, as well as collaborations in pangenomics and population-scale genomic analyses.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Pangenome graph construction (multi-assembly, ONT & PacBio HiFi)
- Structural variant calling & population-scale genotyping (short + long reads)
- GWAS and functional prioritization of candidate loci
- Quantitative genetics & genomic prediction (BLUPF90)
- Reference bias and tropical adaptation in indicine (Zebu) cattle
- Machine learning and BI applied to animal breeding
